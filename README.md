# 宜子醫案（京都大學富士川文庫 キ/20） / Gishi ian [Medical Case Records]: Transcription Dataset

《宜子醫案》是京都大學富士川文庫藏臨床醫案抄本，館方著錄宮崎立伯著。正文按風、寒、咳嗽、內傷、婦人和小兒等門記錄個別病人、症候、治療與複診，並含「狂病」等歷史病名。有和文假名夾注、葉下方名及朱筆訓點，可據以研究漢文醫案在日本的實際編排與讀寫。

Gishi ian is a manuscript of individual case records in Kyoto University’s Fujikawa Collection, attributed to Miyazaki 立伯. The main text records patients, symptoms, treatment choices and follow-up across chapters that include acute conditions, chronic complaints, women’s and children’s cases, and a historical category of madness (kyōbyō). Japanese notes, rubrics and marginal prescription titles accompany the Literary Chinese text.

來源共50幅，資料包括目次、正文、小字旁注、朱筆記事與可辨印記。逐幅定位左右葉，葉下方名分層保存。

All 50 source images are indexed with right- and left-leaf anchors for the contents, case records, small writing, marginalia and visible seals. Prescription titles in the lower margins are identified separately.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

逐幅回圖，比對獨立讀稿及原初錄，採用 96 項回圖訂正。疑讀、藏印或細小批注按實際可辨範圍保留。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。漢文、和文及同欄混寫保留不同標籤。難辨處、訓點與紙損按所見層次標記。正文讀序不跨欄猜補。

Source-image review compared independent observations with the initial transcription and records 96 image-supported changes. Uncertain readings remain marked. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Literary Chinese, Japanese and mixed columns are labelled by the observed writing. Difficult readings, reading marks and physical damage are reported as observed.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
